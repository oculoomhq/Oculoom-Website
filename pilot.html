FAQs

What does the fee waiver cover?

OCULOOM’s professional fee is waived for selected pilot businesses. External costs—such as advertising, paid tools, creator fees or production expenses—will be discussed and agreed before work begins.

How are businesses selected?

We review your business challenge, priorities and the information available to support the work. A short introductory conversation helps us assess whether there is a focused opportunity we can realistically address together within 30 days.

Are specific results guaranteed?

No specific revenue, customer acquisition or growth result is guaranteed. Before starting, we agree on the scope, baseline and success measures. At the end, we review what happened, what we learned and what we recommend next.

What happens after 30 days?

You receive a review of outcomes, learnings and recommendations. If further work makes sense for both sides, we can discuss a separate paid engagement. There is no automatic commitment to continue.

Will our work be used as a case study?

We would ask for your permission before publishing your business name, logo, results, testimonial or project details. Any case-study use would be agreed separately.
