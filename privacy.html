01

About this policy

This draft describes the planned handling of information shared with OCULOOM through oculoom.in, including enquiries and 30-Day Pilot applications. Privacy contact: oculoomhq@gmail.com. The responsible operating entity details must be confirmed before launch.

02

Information you provide

Forms collect your name, email, business name, message, selected topic and, for pilot applications, website or social profile and capability of interest. Do not submit passwords, payment details or confidential customer records.

03

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, assess pilot applications, arrange conversations and administer agreed work. Submitting a form does not subscribe you to promotional email.

04

Technical information & cookies

Netlify hosts the website and may process technical request logs. Optional Google Analytics 4 (G-25L264DXWL) is configured to load only after you accept analytics and only on oculoom.in. We do not intentionally send names, emails or messages to Analytics. You can change your choice using Cookie settings. Browser preference storage is used to remember that choice. Analytics retention and international processing details require review before launch.

05

Service providers

The planned setup uses Netlify for hosting and form storage and the email service supporting oculoomhq@gmail.com for correspondence. If notifications, additional integrations or other processors are enabled, this policy must be updated to reflect their role and processing locations.

06

Retention & security

Unsuccessful pilot applications will be deleted within 10 days after the decision not to proceed, subject to applicable legal requirements. The rejection status workflow and deletion schedule must be configured and tested before applications open. Copies in emails, exports and other systems must also be managed. Retention periods for general enquiries and successful engagements remain to be agreed. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

07

Your requests

Email oculoomhq@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion, withdraw consent where applicable, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity. Requests are handled in accordance with applicable law.

08

External links

LinkedIn, Instagram and other external websites have their own privacy policies. A tool logo on this website does not mean that tool collects visitor data.

09

Changes

We may update this policy when our practices change. An effective date will be set when the final policy is approved for launch.
