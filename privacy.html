01

About this policy

OCULOOM is the Udyam-registered enterprise operating oculoom.in and handling the personal information described here. This policy covers website enquiries, 30-Day Founding Partner Pilot applications and related communications. For privacy requests or complaints, contact oculoomhq@gmail.com.

02

Information you provide

We collect the information you enter in our forms: name, email address, business name, message and selected topic. Pilot applications also ask for a website or social profile and may include your capability of interest. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, sensitive personal information or confidential customer records. Our services are intended for business users, not children.

03

How we use information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, assess pilot suitability, arrange conversations, deliver agreed services and maintain relevant business communications. We also use technical information to operate the website, prevent spam and investigate misuse. Submitting an enquiry or application does not subscribe you to promotional email. Any optional marketing subscription would be requested separately.

04

Technical information & cookies

Netlify hosts the website and may process IP addresses, browser details and request logs for delivery and security. Optional Google Analytics 4 loads only after you accept analytics on our live domain. It helps us understand page visits and successful form submissions. We do not intentionally send names, emails or message contents to Analytics. Our Google tag is configured to set analytics cookies for up to 60 days without renewing that duration on new activity. GA4 user-level and event-level retention is set to two months with reset on new activity off; this does not limit all aggregated reporting. Cookie lifetime and Analytics retention are separate settings. Your analytics preference is stored in your browser until you change it or clear browser storage. Use Cookie settings in the footer to change your choice. Declining analytics does not prevent enquiries or applications. See how Google uses information from websites.

05

Service providers & sharing

We use Netlify for hosting, form storage and the pilot rejection workflow, and Google Gmail for enquiries and form-notification emails. Google Analytics processes optional usage data after consent. Authorised people working for OCULOOM may access information as needed to handle your request or agreed work. We do not sell your personal information. Information may also be disclosed where required by law. These providers may process information outside India, including in the United States and other countries where they operate. Their terms and safeguards apply to that processing. See Netlify’s privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy. A tool listed on our website is not automatically used to collect visitor information.

06

Retention & security

Unsuccessful pilot applications are deleted within 10 days after the decision not to proceed, unless retention is required by law. General enquiries that do not become client engagements are deleted six months after the last conversation. Successful pilot and client project records are retained for 12 months after the engagement ends, unless needed for agreed ongoing work or applicable legal obligations. Financial and tax records follow their separate legal requirements. These periods cover copies under our control, including relevant email notifications and exports. We use manual review and, where configured, automated deletion to carry out these rules. We use reasonable access controls and security safeguards, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

07

Your choices & requests

You can email oculoomhq@gmail.com to request access to, correction of or deletion of your information, withdraw consent where applicable, or raise a privacy complaint. We may need to verify your identity and clarify the request. We handle requests under applicable law and explain any legal reason that prevents deletion. Withdrawal does not undo prior lawful processing and may limit our ability to continue handling an enquiry or engagement that needs the information. Please contact us before sharing another person’s information.

08

External links

Links to LinkedIn, Instagram and other external websites open services governed by their own privacy policies. OCULOOM does not control how those services handle information after you visit them.

09

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or our practices change. We will publish the revised wording and update the date shown above. For questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact oculoomhq@gmail.com.
